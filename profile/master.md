# Master fact bank

The single source of truth for every claim that may appear on a tailored CV. Nothing goes on a CV
that isn't in here. If a job description asks for something absent from this file, that is a gap to
report — not a line to write.

This file deliberately holds several times more material than fits on one page. That surplus is the
whole mechanism: tailoring selects and re-angles from it, so the language on every tailored CV was
already Francois's own. Nothing is composed toward a posting.

**Entry format.** Each entry carries the underlying fact, its quantification, relevance tags, and
one or two alternate phrasings that say the same true thing with different emphasis. Tailoring picks
the phrasing that matches the posting's vocabulary — it does not write new ones from scratch.

## Meridian — Credit Risk Analyst

> Employer name is recorded in `references/immutables.md` as **Meridian Financial Group (Private
> Real Estate Mortgage Fund)**, per the baseline CV. See open question OQ-1 below.
>
> The Managing Director's name was given in interview but is deliberately not recorded here. It has
> no tailoring value — a CV says "presented to the Managing Director", never a name — and this file
> is committed to a remote repository.

### M1 — Underwriting volume and pace
**Fact.** Analyses live lending opportunities at a pace of roughly one deal per day, assessing each
against LTV, LTC, and DSCR thresholds.
**Quantified.** ~1 deal per day. Ticket sizes $400k–$5M. Typical LTV ~50%.
**Tags.** credit, underwriting, volume, risk metrics
**Phrasings.**
- "Underwrite roughly one lending opportunity per day, sizing debt against collateral and testing
  each deal against LTV, LTC, and DSCR thresholds."
- "Assess live loan applications at a rate of ~1 per day across tickets from \$400k to \$5M,
  typically lending at ~50% LTV."
- "Screen and underwrite deal flow of approximately one opportunity daily for a private real estate
  mortgage fund."

### M2 — Asset mix and lending specialism
**Fact.** The book skews toward construction and development lending, with some land and
residential exposure.
**Tags.** construction lending, development finance, real estate credit
**Phrasings.**
- "…across a book weighted toward construction and development lending, with land and residential
  exposure alongside."
- "Underwrite construction and development facilities, plus land and residential lending."

**Note for tailoring.** This is materially more specific than the baseline CV's "residential and
commercial exposures", and it is a genuine specialism — construction/development credit is a
distinct discipline from generalist real estate lending. Lead with it for any development finance,
construction lending, or debt-fund posting.

### M3 — Credit models
**Fact.** Builds credit and cash flow models in Excel using Meridian's existing templates,
frequently adapted to the specifics of each deal.
**Tags.** financial modelling, Excel, credit analysis
**Phrasings.**
- "Build and adapt Excel credit models per deal, covering borrower financial review, collateral
  assessment, and cash flow analysis."
- "Model borrower cash flows and collateral coverage in Excel, adapting the fund's templates to the
  structure of each transaction."

**Honesty marker.** Models are template-based and adapted, **not** built from scratch. Do not write
"built the fund's modelling framework" or similar. "Adapt", "extend", and "build on" are accurate;
"designed" and "created from scratch" are not.

### M4 — Stress testing
**Fact.** Stress-tests deals against cost overruns, price and value decline, and construction
delays.
**Tags.** stress testing, sensitivity analysis, downside risk, construction risk
**Phrasings.**
- "Stress deals against cost overrun, value decline, and construction delay scenarios to quantify
  downside before committing capital."
- "Run sensitivity analysis on build cost, exit pricing, and programme slippage."

**Note.** These three variables are construction-specific, which reinforces M2. A generalist lender
stresses rates and vacancy; a construction lender stresses cost, price, and programme.

### M5 — Site visits and construction monitoring
**Fact.** Conducts site visits as part of underwriting and ongoing monitoring — initially alongside
the Managing Director, now independently.
**Tags.** construction monitoring, autonomy, asset management, site work
**Phrasings.**
- "Conduct site visits independently as part of underwriting and ongoing construction monitoring."
- "Carry out on-site inspections across the construction book, having taken the work over from the
  Managing Director."

**Note.** The progression from accompanied to independent is evidence of trust and autonomy — worth
surfacing for postings that stress working independently or owning a process end to end.

### M6 — Active portfolio intervention
**Fact.** Where a build is at risk of running out of funds, advises borrowers on phased completion
strategy and flags budget shortfalls for additional funding.
**Tags.** asset management, workout, borrower relations, problem loans, construction monitoring
**Phrasings.**
- "Identify budget shortfalls on live construction loans and advise borrowers on phased completion
  strategies to protect the fund's position."
- "Monitor construction draw progress against budget, escalating shortfalls for additional funding
  and restructuring completion sequencing where required."

**Note.** This is post-close asset management, not underwriting — a distinct competency, and one
most graduate candidates have no exposure to at all. High value.

### M7 — Default, enforcement, and recovery
**Fact.** Assisted with a loan default that required court action to obtain power of sale, and was
involved in the subsequent property sale process. Further exposure to loan workouts and
enforcement.
**Tags.** default, enforcement, workout, recovery, special situations, legal process
**Phrasings.**
- "Supported a loan default through court proceedings to obtain power of sale, and worked on the
  subsequent disposal of the security."
- "Gained direct exposure to enforcement and recovery, including a default taken through court to
  power of sale and the onward property sale."
- "Worked loan workouts and enforcement actions alongside performing-book underwriting."

**Note.** The single most differentiating fact in this section. Underwriting experience is common on
graduate CVs; having seen a loan fail, go to court, and be recovered is rare. Lead with it for any
credit risk, special situations, distressed, restructuring, or workout posting. Use "assisted with"
and "supported" — the interview answer was explicit that this was a supporting role.

### M8 — The 34-unit development
**Fact.** Underwrote a 34-unit residential development. Executed the commercial workstream
alongside the principal, owning pricing strategy, absorption assumptions, strata budget, and
go-to-market plan. First building is now complete and on the market.
**Quantified.** 34 units. GDV **$38M**. Price points **$825k–$1.3M**.
**Tags.** development, underwriting, pricing, absorption, go-to-market, transactions
**Phrasings.**
- "Underwrote a 34-unit residential development with a \$38M GDV, owning pricing strategy across
  \$825k–\$1.3M price points, absorption assumptions, and the go-to-market plan."
- "Executed the commercial workstream on a \$38M GDV residential scheme alongside the principal;
  first building now complete and on the market."
- "Set pricing and absorption assumptions on a 34-unit scheme, with the first building delivered
  and now selling."

**Note.** The baseline CV omits the \$38M GDV, which is the largest and most credible figure
available anywhere in this file — larger than the \$20m+ book. Surface it for development,
investment, and transaction-facing roles. "First building complete and on the market" converts the
claim from projected to delivered, which is worth more than the number alone.

### M9 — Presenting to the decision-maker
**Fact.** Presents own analysis and recommendations directly to the Managing Director ahead of the
board's credit decision.
**Tags.** credit committee, recommendation, communication, seniority exposure
**Phrasings.**
- "Present analysis and lending recommendations directly to the Managing Director ahead of board
  approval."
- "Make and defend credit recommendations to the Managing Director, feeding the board's decision."

**Note.** He recommends; the board decides. Do not write "approved" or "made credit decisions".
"Recommend", "present", and "feed into" are accurate. This is nonetheless strong — direct access to
the decision-maker at analyst level is unusual.

### M10 — Accounting for affiliated entities
**Fact.** Maintains full-cycle accounting and bookkeeping for 3 affiliated client entities — a
restaurant, a boat company, and TCI Rugby Union.
**Quantified.** 3 entities, combined revenue in the several-millions.
**Tags.** accounting, bookkeeping, operations, QuickBooks
**Phrasings.**
- "Maintain full-cycle accounting for 3 affiliated entities across hospitality, marine, and
  sport, with combined revenue in the several millions."
- "Run bookkeeping and management accounts for three operating companies alongside the credit
  workload."

**Note.** Lowest relevance to credit and investment postings — usually the first bullet to cut for
space. Keep it for roles that value breadth, small-team environments, or accounting foundations.

### M11 — Strata budget and chart of accounts
**Fact.** Built the strata budget and chart of accounts for a development strata.
**Tags.** development, budgeting, accounting, operations
**Phrasings.**
- "Built the strata budget and chart of accounts for a residential development."

**Note.** Small but specific, and it pairs naturally with M8 — the same scheme's operating side.
Useful filler when a posting values hands-on development detail.

### M12 — Tools
**Fact.** Excel and QuickBooks.
**Tags.** tools
**Note.** No Argus, no CoStar, no Bloomberg, no loan management system. This is a hard boundary. Any
posting requiring those is a bucket-C gap and must be reported as such.

baseline CV reads **"Meridian Financial Group (Private Real Estate Mortgage Fund)"**. Employer names

the 2025 summer stint, or both? Affects whether the figure can be stated unqualified.

**OQ-3 — Annualised volume.** "~1 deal per day" implies roughly 200–250 opportunities a year. That
extrapolation is not yet confirmed and is not to be used until it is.
